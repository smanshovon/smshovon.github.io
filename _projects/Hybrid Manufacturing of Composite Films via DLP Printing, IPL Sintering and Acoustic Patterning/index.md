title: Hybrid Manufacturing of Composite Films via DLP Printing, IPL Sintering, and Acoustic Patterning.
description:  In this project, a hybrid manufacturing approach was taken to fabricate composite thin films to mitigate the limitations of individual manufacturing techniques. A combination of DLP printing, Intense Pulsed Light (IPL) sintering and acoustic patterning was applied to fabricate metal-polymer composite thin films. The performance was evaluated via the electrical conductivity measurements, which showed promising results (from Gigaohms to ohms resistance) when applying hybrid manufacturing techniques.
