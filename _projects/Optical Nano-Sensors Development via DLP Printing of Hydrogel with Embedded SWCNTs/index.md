# Optical Nano-Sensors Development via DLP Printing of Hydrogel with Embedded SWCNTs

This project involved the creation of a DLP-printable hydrogel system infused with functionalized single-walled carbon nanotubes (SWCNTs) to develop optical nanosensor structures with useful sensing capability.

## Objective

The goal was to develop an accessible, printable material platform capable of combining hydrogel mechanics with optical response from embedded SWCNTs for sensing applications.

## Research focus

- hydrogel formulation development
- DLP printability and curing behavior
- SWCNT integration and dispersion
- mechanical characterization
- material diagnostics using FTIR and analysis workflows

The work established a functional route for integrating nanomaterial-enabled sensing with printed hydrogel structures for advanced materials development.
